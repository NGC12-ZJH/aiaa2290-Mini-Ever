# Presentation Guidelines & Evaluation Rubric

> Source: course rubric `Prentation_Guidelines&Evaluation.pdf` (AIAA 2290, EPS in AI).

## 1. Presentation Guidelines

### 1.1 Timing

- The presentation should be **10 minutes** long. A **timekeeper** will remind you at the **8-minute mark** that you have **2 minutes left**.
- If you have not finished by the **10-minute mark**, you will be asked to **conclude your presentation within two sentences**.

### 1.2 Content

- Your presentation should focus on **Significance**, **Originality**, **Diversity**, and **Workload**. Ensure that your content:
  - Demonstrates the **importance** of the topic.
  - Showcases **original thinking** and ideas.
  - Reflects **diversity**, considering different perspectives or including diverse elements.
  - Displays an adequate **workload**, meaning it should have depth without being overly complex or too simple.
- **Goal:** Effectively communicate the relevance of your topic to the audience.

### 1.3 Presentation

- **No Notes Allowed:** You must deliver your presentation **without reading from notes**. Practice to ensure fluency and natural delivery.
- **Introduction to the Topic:** Clearly introduce your topic, stating its significance and relevance.
- **Organization and Logic:** Your presentation should follow a **clear structure**. Begin with an introduction, then move logically through key points, and end with a strong conclusion.
- **Clarity of Presentation:** Ensure that your points are **clearly articulated** and easy to follow. Avoid jargon unless explained.
- **Fluency of Presentation:** Speak confidently and smoothly without unnecessary pauses. Practice the flow of your speech.

### 1.4 Q&A Session

After the presentation, you will face a **Q&A session**. Be prepared to:

- **Answer questions clearly** and with appropriate detail.
- Organize your responses in a structured manner.
- Ensure that your answers are **accurate** and **relevant** to the questions asked.
- Engage in a respectful **interaction** with the questioners.
- Keep responses **concise** yet informative.

## 2. Scoring Rubric (7-Point Likert Scale)

Each criterion is scored on a **7-point Likert scale**:

| Score | Level | Description |
|:-----:|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Poor | Performance is **significantly below** expectations and needs substantial improvement. |
| 2 | Below Average | Performance is **below** expectations and could use significant improvement. |
| 3 | Borderline | Performance is **just acceptable**, but there are notable areas for improvement. |
| 4 | Up Average | Performance is **above average**, meeting most expectations. |
| 5 | Good | Performance is **good**, with few areas for improvement. |
| 6 | Very Good | Performance is **very good**, meeting high standards with only minor areas for improvement. |
| 7 | Excellent | Performance is **outstanding**, exceeding expectations in all aspects. |

All 12 criteria are scored 1–7.
